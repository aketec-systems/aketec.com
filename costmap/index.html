Send us one process. Get back a cost map.

Pick the one that eats the most time — the report somebody rebuilds every month, the re-keying between two systems, the approval that sits in an inbox. Describe it in a few sentences. We send back a written page telling you what it costs you a year.

What comes back

  1. Where the hours go

    The steps as they actually run, who touches each one, and how often — not the version in the process document.

  2. What it costs per year

    Those hours priced at a loaded rate you confirm, so the number is yours and you can defend it to whoever controls the budget.

  3. What is worth fixing first

    Ranked by cost removed against effort to remove it. Some steps are expensive and easy. Some are expensive and not worth touching yet.

  4. What we could not verify

    Every assumption behind the numbers, stated plainly. You should be able to check our work, and disagree with it.

What it costs you Nothing, and about five minutes of typing. We do not need access to your systems.
