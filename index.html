Core competencies

Four things we do, and nothing we don't

Every engagement starts from the same premise: an agency cannot govern, secure, or automate data it cannot account for. We fix the accounting first.

Legacy application modernization

Moving aging systems and the data inside them onto maintainable platforms — without losing lineage, breaking statutory retention, or stranding the records that matter. We work from a documented current-state assessment, not a rewrite proposal.

  • Current-state assessment and technical debt inventory
  • Data migration with lineage preservation
  • Incremental replatforming and strangler-pattern cutover
  • Retention and disposition mapping

Data governance & AI readiness

Most agencies are being asked to adopt AI on top of data nobody has inventoried, classified, or quality-checked. We build the foundation that makes the AI question answerable — and document it in a form that survives an audit.

  • Data inventory, classification, and lineage mapping
  • AI and automated-decision system inventories
  • Governance gap registers and remediation sequencing
  • Data quality assessment and remediation

Security engineering & assessment

Application-layer security for the systems we and others build: review, secure development practice, and remediation support. Findings come with the code change that fixes them, not just a severity rating.

  • Application security review and threat modeling
  • Secure development lifecycle implementation
  • Remediation engineering and verification
  • Access control and audit logging design

Verifiable records engineering

Our specialty. When a record's integrity has to be demonstrable rather than asserted, we design the cryptographic machinery that proves it — tamper-evident logging, provenance chains, and independently verifiable audit trails.

  • Tamper-evident logging and append-only ledgers
  • Cryptographic provenance and chain of custody
  • Credential and document verification systems
  • Distributed ledger architecture where it is warranted

Differentiators

Why a small firm is the right call here

We are not the low-risk choice for a hundred-million-dollar program, and we do not bid those. For scoped, technical, accountable work, the things below are advantages rather than compromises.

01

The engineers who scope the work do the work

There is no bench of junior staff waiting behind the proposal. The senior engineer who assesses your system is the one who writes the remediation. Nothing is handed off after award, because there is nobody to hand it to.

02

Fixed price, fixed scope, documented deliverable

Our assessments are priced and bounded before we start. You know what the document contains and when it arrives. If the work needs to grow, that is a new engagement with its own price — not a change order against an open clock.

03

Deliverables written to your frameworks, not ours

Assessment output is formatted against the modernization and governance frameworks your agency already reports under. That means it can be attached to a funding request or an audit response without translation.

04

Verifiable-records depth most integrators don't have

Cryptographic provenance and tamper-evident system design are a specialty, not a slide. When a records system has to prove its own integrity — for audit, for litigation, or because the statute says so — that is the problem we are built around.

05

Texas-based delivery, US persons only

All work is performed in the United States by US persons. No offshore development, no subcontracted delivery to unnamed third parties. Where a contract carries citizenship or data residency requirements, we already meet them.
