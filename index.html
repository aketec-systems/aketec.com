Where operations lose time

Most of what an operation costs isn't the software — it's the hours around it.

  • Staff answering the same questions over and over
  • Copying information from emails into another system
  • Scheduling and rescheduling appointments by hand
  • Re-entering the same customer information twice
  • Building the same spreadsheet report every month
  • Onboarding each new customer manually
  • Chasing follow-ups that should send themselves
  • Moving data between two systems that do not talk to each other
  • Running an internal process through an email thread

If your team does the same computer task over and over, there is probably an opportunity to automate it.
