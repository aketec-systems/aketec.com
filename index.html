Services

Four things we do

Most engagements combine two or three. We start by understanding the work, not by picking a technology.

AI Automation & Process Engineering

We map how work actually moves through your operation — including the manual bridges between the systems you already pay for — and build the automation that removes them. AI where it genuinely fits, simpler tools where it doesn't.

  • Process mapping and manual-effort measurement
  • Workflow automation and system-to-system handoffs
  • Document and data processing pipelines
  • Predictive and analytical models where data supports them

Custom Software Development

When off-the-shelf software doesn't fit the way you work, we build what does — internal tools, customer-facing applications, and the interfaces that connect them. Built to be maintained, documented so someone else could pick it up.

  • Web and mobile application development
  • Internal tools, portals and dashboards
  • API design and integration layers
  • Modernization and replacement of legacy applications

Digital Infrastructure & Integration

The layer everything else depends on: databases, cloud environments, and the connections between the systems you already run. Most automation projects fail here first, so this is where we look first.

  • Cloud environments on AWS, Azure and GCP
  • Database design, migration and administration
  • Systems integration across existing platforms
  • Access control, backup and operational resilience

Data & Records Engineering

Our technical specialty, and the reason the rest holds up. Data warehousing, lineage and governance — plus cryptographic audit trails for records whose integrity has to be demonstrable rather than asserted.

  • ETL pipelines and data warehousing
  • Data inventory, classification and lineage
  • Tamper-evident logging and cryptographic provenance
  • Reporting and business intelligence

Workflow Cost Audit

Our entry point for businesses. We sit with the people doing the work, map the processes end to end, and count the hours going into repetitive steps and system-to-system bridging. You get a costed picture of that work — including the software you're paying for and not fully using — a ranked list of what could be automated, and what each fix would take to build.

You receive Process maps, manual-effort inventory, opportunity register ranked by payback, costed roadmap

AI Use & Governance Readiness Review

For agencies and regulated organizations. We catalogue every AI and automated decision system already operating — including the ones embedded in vendor products nobody catalogued as AI — and map each against the obligations that apply to you.

Legacy System & Modernization Assessment

For organizations carrying an aging system nobody wants to touch. We assess its technical risk, the condition of the data inside it, and the realistic options — written so the output drops into a budget request without translation.

01

The engineer who scopes the work does the work

02

Automation is easy to sell and easy to get wrong. We measure what the manual work costs before proposing anything, so the decision to build rests on your numbers rather than our enthusiasm.

03
